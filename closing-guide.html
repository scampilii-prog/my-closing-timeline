Important situations during a transaction

Closing date needs to change — what next?

Confirm why the change is needed, who must agree and whether a written amendment or contractual extension provision applies. Obtain the necessary documentation and approvals. Update your Timeline only after confirming the actual changed date.

An inspection or financing deadline is approaching

Check the exact contract language and calendar-counting rules; notify the appropriate parties promptly, obtain client instructions and coordinate any required written notice. A reminder does not itself satisfy a notice requirement.

The HOA or condominium approval is delayed

Request a status update and document outstanding requirements. Check the contract and association requirements for consequences and options. Do not assume the delay automatically changes closing.

The client wants to send closing funds

Warn clients about wire fraud. Independently confirm wiring instructions with the verified closing agent using a trusted number — never rely solely on an email, text or changed instructions.

How private PRO access will work

1 · Create your account

The Realtor registers and verifies their account before accessing any protected transaction.

2 · Pay for a transaction

The planned price is $20 for each new transaction. A confirmed payment activates access to that transaction.

3 · Open My Transactions

After login, the Realtor sees only transactions authorized for that account, with daily tasks and closing milestones.

How private PRO access will work

1 · Create your account

The Realtor registers and verifies their account before accessing any protected transaction.

2 · Pay for a transaction

The planned price is $20 for each new transaction. A confirmed payment activates access to that transaction.

3 · Open My Transactions

After login, the Realtor sees only transactions authorized for that account, with daily tasks and closing milestones.

Built around the daily workflow

Buyer Agent

Escrow, inspection, financing, appraisal if applicable, insurance, association approval, walkthrough and closing.

Listing Agent

Executed documents, escrow confirmation, inspection access, agreed repairs, title, payoffs, HOA documents and possession.

Today's priorities

Tasks due today, tomorrow, overdue and upcoming, with a completed-action history once secure storage is operational.

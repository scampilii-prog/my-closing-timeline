BEFORE YOU START

Your closing questions, clearly answered.

Practical answers to help you choose FREE or PRO and organize your dates with confidence.

What does Real Estate Closing Timeline do?

It organizes contract deadlines and follow-up tasks in one timeline. The signed agreement and applicable requirements—not this tool—control the actual deadlines.

Can I try it without paying?

Yes. FREE lets you practice with suggested contract tasks and create a timeline without purchasing a PRO credit. FREE work is not saved as a PRO closing.

What is included with PRO?

One PRO credit starts one registered closing with a saved timeline, contract deadlines, follow-up checklist and Today & Tomorrow overview. A private fillable PDF is available for your own records.

How much does Real Estate PRO cost?

$20 per closing for up to 60 days. Eligible new accounts can buy two closing credits for $35 during the first 30 days after registration. Purchased credits do not expire when the introductory offer ends.

Does Real Estate PRO automatically renew?

No. Real Estate PRO uses a credit per closing rather than a monthly subscription. The $20 credit covers one closing for up to 60 days.

Which dates do I enter first?

Start with the Effective Date and Closing Date from your signed agreement. Review all suggested tasks and deadlines before relying on them.

Can I change a date or mark a task done?

PRO provides controls for updating permitted dates and recording completed tasks. Changes to the Closing Date may recalculate dependent reminders. The Effective Date is intended to remain fixed once the closing starts.

Does the system verify legal deadlines?

No. It is an organizational tool, not legal advice or an automated legal deadline verification service. Check contract provisions, notice requirements, weekends, holidays and relevant state rules with qualified professionals when necessary.

Must I enter client names or property addresses?

No. Use an anonymous closing reference. Keep buyer and seller names, property addresses and other private information in your own records, not in the online timeline.

Can I pay for PRO today?

Checkout currently runs in Stripe Sandbox for simulated test payments only. Real charges are not enabled yet. You can still use the FREE practice timeline.

    Understanding your closing timeline

    Florida transaction guide — always verify your signed contract.

    1. Contract & escrow

    Confirm effective date, deposits and escrow instructions.

    2. Inspection period

    Coordinate inspections and written notices.

    3. Financing & appraisal

    Track lender conditions and appraisal when applicable.

    4. Title & insurance

    Review title matters, liens, survey and insurance.

    5. HOA & condominium

    Check applications, approvals and documents.

    6. Final preparations

    Track disclosures, walkthrough, funding and closing.

    Important: Planning information only, not legal advice. Contract terms, addenda and applicable rules control deadlines and extensions.

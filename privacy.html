Privacy and use

No private account yet

This public preview has no account sign-in and does not store transaction data on our servers.

Local exports

Downloaded CSV files and printed PDFs are handled by your device. The Prepare Email control opens your configured email application and does not send mail itself.

Do not enter sensitive data

Use illustrative or non-sensitive property details while testing. Do not enter IDs, bank account details or private financial information.

Educational use

Timelines are user-entered planning aids. They do not establish contract deadlines or replace legal advice.

Future service

Paid accounts, payments, secure storage, data retention and separate service terms will require additional policies before launch.

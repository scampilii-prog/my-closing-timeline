1 · Privacy Policy — What information we process

FREE Timeline. Visitors can enter an anonymous closing reference, tasks and dates without creating a PRO account. The public timeline no longer requests buyer/seller names, property addresses or purchase prices. User-written notes and custom tasks still require care: do not type identifying details into them. The public calculation experience runs in the browser; it is not designed to upload its entries into the PRO closing database. If you copy, print, download or open an email draft, the resulting information is handled by your browser, device or chosen email app. The private fillable PDF is created locally in the browser, and the personal fields are blank when downloaded. Complete them in your device PDF reader; those subsequent entries are not transmitted to our site. Browser settings (such as language) and some demonstration or owner-only workspace preferences may use local browser storage. Do not assume information remains available after you leave.

PRO accounts, if enabled. Registration requests your name, email and password; account services can store verification and session records, credit balances, purchases, invitations, private closing reference codes, effective/closing dates, task details, document-name templates and transaction activity in Cloudflare D1. New PRO closings are designed to reject property addresses; however, legacy projects created before the privacy update may still contain names or property addresses until they are securely reviewed and removed. Passwords are stored as derived hashes rather than plain-text passwords. Your browser also receives a secure, HttpOnly session cookie when signed in.

First-free invitations and marketing. Requesting an invitation requires an email address and stores a request status, confirmation, and any issued private code reference separately from PRO closing details. The email is used to verify and administer the invitation. A separate, unchecked opt-in is offered for optional product news and promotions; refusing does not affect eligibility. Promotional consent is recorded only after the address is verified and can be withdrawn using Unsubscribe. Service and security emails may still be sent. Personal closing information is not collected by the invitation form.

Service operations. Hosting and security systems may process IP addresses, device/browser information, technical logs and request metadata to deliver and protect the website. Contact, account recovery, email verification or invitation messages may involve the email address and content supplied.

Payment testing. PRO checkout currently uses Stripe Sandbox only. No live card charges should occur on this site while this configuration remains in place. If live checkout launches, payment details will be processed by the configured payment provider, and this notice and checkout disclosures must be reviewed and updated first.

Account and transaction records may remain until deleted following a verified request, an applicable retention requirement or legitimate operational need. No fixed universal deletion schedule is currently promised. Stored information should be limited to what is necessary, and retention practices must be reviewed before full launch.

7 · Terms of Use — What the service does and does not do

My Closing Timeline is a planning, organization and communication aid, not a law firm, legal service, licensed escrow company, title insurer, lender, or substitute for a licensed professional. It does not review the validity of a signed contract, deliver legally effective notices on your behalf, guarantee that a deadline is correct, verify state holidays or local rules, or promise a completed closing.

You control the underlying transaction. Users must confirm dates, day-counting rules, notice requirements, contract amendments, financing milestones, local law, deadlines and time zones against the executed contract and professional guidance. Users must be authorized to handle any information entered, respect applicable confidentiality, privacy and professional obligations, keep their login credentials private, and refrain from unlawful or misleading use.

About My Closing Timeline

Built for Realtors. Useful for buyers and sellers.

My Closing Timeline is a Florida Costiera project created to simplify the everyday organization of real estate transactions.

Florida-focusedClear transaction milestonesFree resources

Our purpose

Simple by design

Give agents an accessible, understandable place to organize milestones without unnecessary complexity.

Built to complement Florida Costiera

The real estate website and the closing timeline application remain separate projects, linked by helpful resources.

Florida first

Start with Florida residential transactions and expand only after templates, compliance and usability have been reviewed.

Important limits

Not a substitute for professional judgment

The tool does not interpret contracts or create legally binding deadlines.

No agency relationship by browsing

Using this educational tool does not by itself create an agency relationship with Florida Costiera or its associated professionals.

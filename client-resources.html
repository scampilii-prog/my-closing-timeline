Florida-focusedClear transaction milestonesFree resources

For buyers

After signing

Confirm deposits, financing and inspection arrangements with your agent.

Before closing

Provide requested lender documentation, arrange insurance, and plan the final walkthrough.

At closing

Review settlement instructions through a trusted verified channel and follow your closing team's directions.

For sellers

After signing

Coordinate agreed access, inspections and any required disclosures or documentation.

During the transaction

Work with the closing team on title questions, associations, payoff requests and agreed repairs.

At closing

Confirm signing instructions, keys, possession terms and disbursement with authorized professionals.

Safety first

Wire fraud

Never rely on changed wiring instructions received by email alone; independently verify using a known phone number.

Private information

Do not send identification, bank statements or other confidential records through a public form or unsecured email.

Date changes

Ask your agent to confirm any change in a contractual deadline in writing.

    Understanding your closing timeline

    Florida transaction guide — always verify your signed contract.

    1. Contract & escrow

    Confirm effective date, deposits and escrow instructions.

    2. Inspection period

    Coordinate inspections and written notices.

    3. Financing & appraisal

    Track lender conditions and appraisal when applicable.

    4. Title & insurance

    Review title matters, liens, survey and insurance.

    5. HOA & condominium

    Check applications, approvals and documents.

    6. Final preparations

    Track disclosures, walkthrough, funding and closing.

    Important: Planning information only, not legal advice. Contract terms, addenda and applicable rules control deadlines and extensions.

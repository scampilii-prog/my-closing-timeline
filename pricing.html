Questions Before You Choose

Is FREE really free?

Yes. FREE is browser-based and does not require a PRO account. It does not provide saved account projects or the separate Step 3 follow-up workspace.

Will a PRO credit charge me every month?

No recurring monthly subscription is configured. PRO is designed around one-time credits; one credit is used when registering a new closing successfully.

Can I pay $19.90, $34.90 or $84.90 today?

No. These are proposed LAUNCH90 prices, currently shown for Sandbox testing. The higher comparison amounts are planned later prices, not historical selling prices. Actual taxes, refund terms and totals must be disclosed before live checkout.

What happens if the contract changes or is cancelled?

Date adjustments to the same registered closing do not use another credit. Cancellation does not automatically restore the used credit. Refunds and mandatory consumer rights must be addressed in the production purchase policy before payment is accepted.

Does the timeline verify legal deadlines across the United States?

No. The app is a planning aid. Verify your state-specific forms, executed contract, counting rules, notice procedures and time zones; use independent reminders and professional advice.

How are my data and legal rights addressed?

Understanding your closing timeline

Florida transaction guide — always verify your specific contract.

1. Contract & escrow

Confirm effective date, deposits and escrow instructions.

2. Inspection period

Coordinate inspections and track any written notices.

3. Financing & appraisal

Track loan conditions, commitment and appraisal when applicable.

4. Title & insurance

Review title issues, liens, survey and required insurance.

5. HOA & condominium

Check association applications, approvals and documents.

6. Final preparations

Track disclosures, final walkthrough, funding and closing.

Important: This is an organizational tool, not legal advice or a deadline calculator. Follow the executed agreement, addenda and applicable law. Dates and requirements differ by transaction. This preview does not store information on a server; do not enter sensitive financial or identification information.

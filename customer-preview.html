STEP 1 · REDEEM ONE PRO CREDIT

Register a New Closing

This form will appear only after the customer signs in and has a valid, unused credit. Registering the property assigns one credit to one transaction.

Upon activation: The closing is associated with a unique project ID, the property and contract dates are recorded, and the full PRO timeline opens below. Ordinary editing cannot reassign the credit to a different property. The original effective date and original closing date remain preserved in the audit record. A signed extension or legitimate correction creates a separately recorded amendment without rewriting the original dates. The optional closing note is informational only and never changes calculated dates.

Follow-up tasksAdd, remove or complete tasks, and schedule personal observations on dates within the authorized period, without changing the registered transaction or locked past task dates.
Closing & extensions. Changes affecting the closing deadline must be filed as an authenticated contract amendment before the current closing cutoff. The original dates and amendment history remain on record. Without a valid extension, editing ends 24 hours after the scheduled closing instant (using the saved closing time and time zone; when time is absent, the system must apply a clearly disclosed default), with the final 24 hours available for last tasks and reports. The project then becomes permanently read-only for the customer, subject to legal record-correction and retention requirements.
Keep the record. After closure the customer can sign in and view, print and download the archived report, but cannot edit or activate it for another property. The production system must enforce all credit, deadline and revision rules on the server, not just in the browser.
